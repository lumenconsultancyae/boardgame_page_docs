# Getting Started with Boardgame Hub — End-User Guide

Welcome to **Boardgame Hub**, your companion web application for tracking board games, logging game night sessions, teaching rules with interactive diagrams, sharing formatted game summaries on WhatsApp, and discovering tabletop events across the UAE.

---

## First-Run Onboarding & Cold-Start Checklist

When opening Boardgame Hub for the first time:
1. **Explore or Populate Your Catalog**:
   - If your catalog starts empty, click **+ Add Game** in the top-right toolbar of **Collection & Plays** to add your first board game with its player range, playtime, complexity weight (`1.0–5.0`), and setup steps.
   - If starter games are pre-loaded (*Catan*, *Wingspan*, *Azul*, *Dune: Imperium*, *Ticket to Ride: Europe*, *Terraforming Mars*), use the toolbar filters to browse by **Status** (`Owned`, `Played`, `Wishlist`), **Player Count**, **Max Playtime**, or **Max Complexity**.
2. **Prepare for Game Night**:
   - Filter your catalog to match tonight's group size (for example, `4 Players` and `Medium` complexity) and click **Share Game List on WhatsApp** to send the candidate list to your group chat.

## Core Workflows

### Managing Your Collection & Logging Play Sessions

1. Open the **Collection & Plays** tab.
2. To add a new game, click **+ Add Game**, enter the **Game Title**, player count bounds, playtime, complexity weight, comma-separated mechanics, and semicolon-separated setup steps, then click **Save Game to Collection**.
3. To record a finished game night session, click **+ Log Play Session**:
   - Select the played **Board Game** from the dropdown.
   - Enter the comma-separated **Players**, the **Winner**, session **Duration**, **Location / Café**, optional **Player Scores** (e.g. `Omar: 10, Sara: 8`), and optional **Session Notes**.
   - Click **Record Play Session**. Your session appears immediately in the **Recent Play Log** and increments the game's play counter.

### Teaching Rules with Visual Flowcharts

1. Click **Rule Diagram** on any game card (or switch to the **Rule Summaries & Diagrams** tab and choose a game from the dropdown).
2. Inspect the **Interactive Setup & Turn-Order Flowchart** at the top of the screen:
   - Click any step box (`Setup`, `Phase 1`, `Phase 2`, ..., `Scoring & Victory`) to highlight that step and view its detailed rules in the inspector bar below the diagram.
3. Review the 3-column rule cheat-sheet covering:
   - **1. Table Setup Checklist**
   - **2. Turn-by-Turn Phase Breakdown**
   - **3. Scoring & Teach Tips**
4. Expand **View Exportable Mermaid v11 Flowchart Syntax** at the bottom if you wish to copy the flowchart diagram into personal notes.

### Analyzing Your Collection Graphs

1. Switch to the **Graphs & Analytics** tab.
2. Review top-level summary metrics (**Total Games in Collection**, **Owned / Wishlist**, **Total Play Count**, **Avg Complexity Weight**, and **Average Rating**).
3. Inspect the four visual distribution charts:
   - **Complexity Weight Distribution** (Light `1.0–2.0`, Medium `2.1–3.2`, Heavy `3.3–5.0`)
   - **Player-Count Coverage** (`1P Solo` through `5P+`)
   - **Core Game Mechanics Graph**
   - **Most-Played Games Leaderboard**

### Discovering & Hosting UAE Boardgame Events

1. Switch to the **UAE Events** tab.
2. Filter upcoming community events across **Dubai**, **Abu Dhabi**, **Sharjah**, **Ajman**, **Ras Al Khaimah**, **Fujairah**, and **Umm Al Quwain**, or search by venue or featured game.
3. Click **RSVP (+1 Seat)** on any event card to reserve a seat and increment the live RSVP counter.
4. To organize your own meetup or tournament, click **+ Host UAE Boardgame Event**, fill in the title, Emirate, venue/café, date, GST start time, max players, and featured games, and click **Publish UAE Event**.

### Sharing on WhatsApp

- Click **WhatsApp Rules** on any game card, **Share Rule Summary on WhatsApp** in the Rules tab, **Share Game List on WhatsApp** in the Collection toolbar, or **Share on WhatsApp** on any UAE event card.
- The **WhatsApp Message Ready to Share** drawer opens with a preview of the formatted message.
- Click **Copy Formatted Text** to copy it to your clipboard, or click **Send via WhatsApp (wa.me)** to open WhatsApp directly with the pre-filled message.
